Bring a friend.

Two players. Two teams. One very unlucky island.

Choose your landscape, worms and energy in the game menu, then host a match. You play team 1; your friend plays team 2.

Rooms stay open for 10 minutes.

The host streams the game and keeps this tab visible. Keyboard and mouse required. If a network blocks the connection, the server needs a TURN relay.
